About

[Draft, for Lucy to rewrite in her own words.] Lucy Nadoban is a senior at Fiorello H. LaGuardia High School of Music & Art and Performing Arts, where she sings in the Senior Chorus and writes for the New Music Ensemble. Her original songs “Dead to Me,” “Ms. Snow” and “Black Tides” premiered at the ensemble’s showcases in 2025 and 2026.

From 2022 to 2026 she sang in the Brooklyn Youth Chorus Concert Ensemble, performing at Carnegie Hall, Radio City Music Hall, David Geffen Hall and the Tokyo Dome. In summer 2026 she studied songwriting at Idyllwild Arts and at UCLA’s Herb Alpert School of Music.

She also writes and plays in Coreadon, an original rock duo.
